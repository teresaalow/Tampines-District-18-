About District 18

Tampines —
Singapore's Eastern Hub

Tampines is one of Singapore's most established and self-contained new towns. With multiple MRT lines, a world-class retail hub, strong schools, and competitive pricing, Tampines consistently attracts owner-occupier families and savvy investors looking for value in the East.

6.3% Below nearby district median PSF — a potential value opportunity
+26.2% Sale PSF rise over last 3 months (2026)
53 Tracked condo projects with transaction data
2 MRT Lines EW Line + Cross Island Line (CRL) coming
🌳

Green Living in the East

Tampines Eco Green, Bedok Reservoir, Tampines Quarry, and the nearby coast give residents abundant green and recreational spaces rarely found in suburban Singapore.

🚇

Exceptional Connectivity

Served by Tampines, Tampines East, and Tampines West MRT stations across two lines (EW & DT). The upcoming Cross Island Line (CRL) adds Tampines North — making Tampines one of the best-connected towns outside the city centre.

🏫

Outstanding School Belt

Home to multiple top primary schools, St. Hilda's Secondary, two junior colleges (Tampines Meridian JC & Temasek JC), and Temasek Polytechnic — ideal for families at every stage.

🛍️

Singapore's Best Suburban Retail

Tampines Mall, Century Square, Tampines 1, IKEA, Courts Megastore, and Our Tampines Hub make Tampines arguably the finest retail and lifestyle suburb in Singapore.

📈

Value Relative to Peers

D18 median PSF sits 6.3% below nearby districts, with sale PSF up 26.2% in the last quarter. The CRL catalyst and Tampines North development add meaningful upside potential.

Grade Breakdown

S

Exceptional

Top-tier MRT access, strong school, healthy lease, high liquidity. Rare — only the best projects qualify.

A

Very Good

Strong fundamentals across most factors. Solid buy for owner-occupiers and investors.

B

Good

Above-average fundamentals. Good for own stay; invest selectively depending on unit stack and price.

C

Average

Trade-offs exist (lease, MRT, or school). Still viable — price accordingly and understand the compromises.

D

Below Average

Significant weaknesses in key factors. Approach with caution — ensure price reflects the risks.
